Meridian is launching **Meridian Field**, an offline-first data collection tool for

## Content

### Opening

**Meridian Field**
Survey anywhere. Sync when you can.

### The problem

Environmental survey teams work where connectivity does not.

Field biologists, hydrologists and air-quality technicians spend their days in
canyons, wetlands and basements. The tools they are given assume a live connection.
So the work happens twice: once on paper in the field, once again at a desk that
night — transcription that costs the average team **6 hours per field day** and
introduces errors nobody catches until the dataset is already in a report.

### The stat that matters

**41%** of survey datasets contain at least one transcription error traceable to
manual re-entry.

*Source: fictional internal study, 2026. Placeholder figure for layout purposes.*

### What we built

Meridian Field is a full survey client that runs entirely offline.

- Complete data capture with no network dependency at any point
- Conflict-aware sync that merges edits from multiple surveyors on reconnect
- Photo, audio and GPS capture attached to every record
- Schema validation at entry time, not after upload

### How it works

Three steps:

1. **Define** your survey schema once, on the web. Field types, validation rules,
   required photos.
2. **Collect** on any device, offline, for as long as the fieldwork takes. Days or weeks.
3. **Sync** when you reach connectivity. Conflicts surface as a review queue, never
   as silent overwrites.

### Proof

Cascade Watershed Alliance ran Meridian Field across a 14-week stream survey with
nine simultaneous surveyors.

- **0** records lost
- **6.5 hrs** saved per field day
- **14 weeks** of continuous offline operation before first sync

> "We stopped doing the evening data-entry shift entirely. That is the whole review."
