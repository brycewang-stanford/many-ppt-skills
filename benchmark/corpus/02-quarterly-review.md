# Corpus 02 — Quarterly Business Review

**Stresses:** data fidelity · charts and tables · high information density · action titles
**Target length:** 12–15 slides
**Audience:** an executive committee reading on laptops in a meeting room; the deck is
circulated afterwards and must stand alone
**Failure modes this corpus exposes:** invented numbers, charts rendered as flat images,
tables that overflow the slide, decorative styling that fights readability, titles that
label ("Q3 Revenue") instead of asserting ("Q3 revenue grew 12% on enterprise renewals")

> All content below is fictional. "Northwind Logistics" is an invented company used solely
> as neutral test material. Every figure is fabricated for layout testing.

## Brief

Northwind Logistics Q3 FY26 business review for the executive committee. Reading-first

### Margin

Gross margin fell from **31.2%** to **29.4%**. Two drivers:

- **Fuel** — up 14% per mile against a flat contracted rate card. Impact: **−110 bps**.
- **Contract labor** — peak-season warehouse staffing 22% above plan. Impact: **−70 bps**.

Operating margin held at **8.1%** because SG&A grew only 3.2% against 12.4% revenue growth.

### Customer concentration is improving

Top-10 customer share fell from **44%** to **38%** year over year — the fourth
consecutive quarter of decline. Net new logos: **31**, against **18** in Q3 FY25.

Churn by count was **4.2%**; churn by revenue was **2.1%**, meaning the accounts we
lost were smaller than average.

### Last-mile is the growth engine but not yet profitable

Last-mile grew 23.4% and now represents 16.7% of revenue, up from 15.2%.
Segment contribution margin remains **−3.4%**, improving from −7.9% a year ago.
Break-even is projected in **Q2 FY27** at current trajectory.

### Risks

1. **Fuel exposure is unhedged beyond Q1 FY27.** A further 10% move costs an estimated
   $1.8M annualized.
2. **Warehouse lease renewals** in Memphis and Reno land in Q4 FY26, covering 340k sq ft
   at rates last set in 2021.
3. **Customs segment decline** is structural, not cyclical — two clients insourced.

### Asks

- Approve **$2.4M** capex for Reno automation, targeting a 15-month payback.
- Approve fuel hedging through Q4 FY27 at up to 60% of projected volume.
- Approve **8 incremental engineering roles** to accelerate the last-mile routing rebuild.

### Q4 outlook

Revenue guidance **$48.5M–$50.0M**. Gross margin expected to recover **60–90 bps**
as peak-season contract labor rolls off.
